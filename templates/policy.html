Privacy & Child Safety Policy

Effective Date: 2026 | Save Drive Operations

1. Introduction & Commitment

Save Drive is dedicated to ensuring the absolute safety, security, and privacy of student passengers and families. Because our platform manages sensitive student transportation logistics and real-time oversight for minors, we adhere to strict ethical and legal standards regarding child safety and data privacy.

2. Minor Data Protection & Privacy

  • Restricted Data Collection: We collect only the minimum necessary information required to coordinate safe student transport, including student names, designated pickup/drop-off points, and verified institutional identifiers.
  • Encryption & Security: All transactional ledgers, student registries, and real-time transit logs are encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is strictly restricted to authorized school principals via secure authentication portals.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Student data is never sold, traded, or shared with unauthorized commercial entities or third parties. Data is used solely for school transit oversight and safety verification.

3. Child Safety & Verification Protocols

  • Identity Verification: No student is permitted to board a transport vehicle without prior verification through the Save Drive institutional registry managed by school authorities.
  • Real-Time Monitoring: Authorized school principals maintain continuous oversight via command dashboards to track active transport phases and verify driver-student pairings.
  • Designated Handlers: Drivers and transport handlers undergo strict vetting and must log all status updates through the platform to maintain an accountable chain of custody for every minor.

4. Emergency & Incident Response

  • Immediate Escalation: In the event of a route deviation, mechanical failure, or safety alert, the system triggers automated notifications to school leadership and designated emergency contacts.
  • Audit Trails: Every safety transaction, status change, and login session is permanently recorded in the immutable Safety Transaction Ledger, ensuring complete accountability for post-incident reviews.

5. User Responsibilities & Account Security

  • Principal & Administrator Accountability: School principals and administrators are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their login credentials and ensuring that active sessions are properly terminated upon exiting.
  • Policy Compliance: Any misuse of the platform, unauthorized data extraction, or violation of child safety protocols will result in immediate account revocation and regulatory escalation.
