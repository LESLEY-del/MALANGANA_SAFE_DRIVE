Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Safe Drive Logistics ("Safe Drive", "we", "us") collects, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on your role, we may collect:

  • Account details: name, username, email, phone number, role, school code
  • Verification data: a verification photo taken at signup; for drivers, a photo of your driver's license
  • Location data: a driver's self-reported operating town/area (not continuous GPS tracking)
  • Transport records: booking requests, trip status, pickup/drop-off timestamps
  • Messages: content sent through in-app chat between users coordinating transport
  • Attendance records: for school-linked students, attendance marked by school moderators/teachers

2. How We Use This Information

  • To verify identity and reduce impersonation or fraudulent driver accounts
  • To connect students/parents with available drivers
  • To track and log transport status for safety and accountability
  • To enable communication between relevant parties (driver, student, parent, school)
  • To notify parents and drivers of school dismissal events ("School Out" alerts)

3. Who Can See Your Information

We limit visibility based on relationship, not open access:

  • A student's transport status and safety logs are visible to that student, their linked parent/guardian, their assigned driver, and (for school transport) their school's principal
  • A driver's profile (name, photo, general operating area) is visible to students and parents browsing for transport
  • License and verification images are visible only to the driver themself and to platform administrators for verification purposes — they are never shown to other students, parents, or drivers
  • Chat messages are visible only to the participants in that conversation

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using industry-standard cloud infrastructure. Passwords are stored using one-way cryptographic hashing (bcrypt) — we cannot see your actual password. Access to sensitive records (license images, chat logs, safety logs) is restricted server-side and is not directly accessible via public keys or unauthenticated requests.

5. Children's Data

Student accounts may belong to minors. We collect only the information necessary to coordinate their safe transport (name, school code, grade, transport status, attendance). We rely on the registering parent/guardian, principal, or school moderator to confirm appropriate consent for a minor's use of the platform. Parents/guardians can request the removal of their child's account and associated data at any time through the platform or by contacting us.

6. Data Retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, associated bookings, chat rooms, and links are removed. Certain safety-relevant logs may be retained longer where necessary for legal, safety, or dispute-resolution purposes.

7. Your Rights

You may:

  • View and update your profile information from your dashboard
  • Delete your profile picture or license image at any time
  • Permanently delete your account, which removes your profile and associated records as described above
  • Contact us to ask what data we hold about you or to request its removal

8. Third-Party Services

We use third-party infrastructure providers (cloud database and file storage) to operate the platform. These providers process data on our behalf and do not use it for their own purposes.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated to users where reasonably possible. Continued use of the platform after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns, contact us at safedrive@gmail.com.
